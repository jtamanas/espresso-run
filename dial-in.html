How the advice works

  • The common starting recipe is a 1:2 ratio (for example 18 g in, 36 g out) in about 25 to 30 seconds.
  • Sour usually means under-extracted: grind finer, or run the shot a little longer.
  • Bitter usually means over-extracted: grind coarser, or stop the shot earlier.
  • Fast shots are usually ground too coarse; slow shots too fine. If the taste disagrees with the clock, the helper suggests checking puck prep for channeling.
  • Taste is the final judge. These are rules of thumb that give you a sensible next shot, not guarantees. Beans, machines and grinders all differ.
